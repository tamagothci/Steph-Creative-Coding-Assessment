description of the six outputs you chose, what language they're in (p5js, touch designer) and why you chose them

1 - Week8audiofeedback week 8 - I created this output by following a tutorial by '
bileam tschepe (elekktronaut)' on YouTube. I chose it because I felt like I learned a lot during the process. During the process of making this output I started to understand how feedback loops work and where to add other TOPs to affect the outcome in different ways. I also find it really satisfying to watch how the 'particles' fall. There's an extra noise TOP in the feedback loop as well as a ramp, slope and blur (however I bypassed the ramp when recording the effects because I didn't like how blown out it made the particles. The feedback loop is closed by a displace which is what blows the particles out towards the sides instead of going straight down. The level TOPs that I've added afterwards affect the saturation and contrast and the TOP's after that are how the frame was made and the colour of the background. I would like to understand CHOP operators more to understand more how they are working together as I deleted my audio from this work because something was wrong and I wasn't able to troubleshoot it.

2 - Wk9_Fireflies week 9 - I chose to submit this touch designer output because I played around for a long time to get the outcome that I wanted. The way that the 'fireflies' kind of stick to one another even though they're going at different speeds surprised me but I really like it. There is a different network for each circle which is made up of a constant (for the background) and a circle + ramp joined together with an over which allowed me to use a small CHOP network with noise and math to dictate where the circle would move on the x and y axis. I changed the math lightly for each circle to make the movement slightly different. After I joined all 4 of those networks together with more copy TOPs, I added a feedback loop with different effect TOPs to get the glowing effect before closing the loop and adding a black background. I could take my learning further by figuring out what operator is causing all of the circles to follow each other. I went back and changed the seed as I just thought of it now but they still seem to bunch up in the middle somewhat so it would be cool to figure that out.
